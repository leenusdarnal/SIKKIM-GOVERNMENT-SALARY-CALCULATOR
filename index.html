Calculation Formula

Components calculated as per Govt. of Sikkim Pay Matrix Table:

  • DA: Selected % of Basic Pay
  • HRA: Max(₹3,500, 12% of Basic Pay)
  • SBCA: 8% of Basic Pay
  • TA: ₹500 (L1-14) / ₹1,000 (L15-18)
  • CPF: 10% of (Basic Pay + DA)
  • Stamp Fee: ₹5.00
  • Professional Tax: ₹150 (Levels 1–14) / ₹200 (Level 15 and above)
