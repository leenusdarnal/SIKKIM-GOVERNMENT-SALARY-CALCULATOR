# Salary / arrears statement audit

The arrears planner provides a print-ready A4 landscape salary / arrears calculation statement for DDO / Accounts review. It is a draft calculation aid, not an official Government certificate, pay-fixation order or arrears sanction.

## Controls
- A statement is available only when the result is valid and complete, has rows, and every month has actual credited pay and a calculated net difference.
- The statement consumes the existing arrears calculation output; no duplicate salary or arrears formula is introduced.
- The audit CSV remains available when inputs are incomplete or blocked.
- Optional employee / office metadata is held in page state only and excluded from local draft autosave and JSON backup.
- Browser Print / Save as PDF is used; the statement is formatted for A4 landscape.

## Contents
Employee / office details; period; month-wise basic, DA, allowances, gross, deductions, revised net, actual credited pay, additional recovery and net difference; totals; entered order references; employee declaration; DDO / Accounts verification.

## Verification
Verify employee-specific eligibility, service-book entries, source orders, payslips, deductions and rounding with the competent DDO / Accounts authority. No official seal, statutory form or sanction authority is implied.
